Backup terms of use

Updated November 7, 2026

This tool is intended only for authorized administration of Fantasy Stork League, using a Google Drive account the operator controls. It is not a public backup service.

The operator must safeguard the recovery key, maintain sufficient storage, monitor backup failures, and verify restoration before relying on a recovery copy. Successful upload alone does not guarantee recoverability. Provider availability, authorization expiry, and free-plan limits can interrupt backups.

The operator can discontinue the tool and revoke its Google authorization at any time. Backup data handling is described in the privacy policy.
