# Expanded bird portraits

Generated with the built-in image tool, one image per call. Original portraits 01, 09 and later 03, 06 supplied as style references. Final assets: public/stork-portraits/stork-65.webp through stork-256.webp (256 × 256 WebP). Original 64 assets preserved.

The first batches used varied profile and three-quarter poses with coral/teal, lavender/plum, teal/gold, ochre/navy, rose/forest, sage/terracotta, cobalt/apricot and rust/slate backgrounds. Later batches explicitly added frontal, over-shoulder, preening and head-tilt poses. IDs 208–223 were recovered in generation order; their prompt recipe specified these natural poses, white head/neck/chest, black folded wings, red-orange tapered bill, comfortable head margins, readability at 44px, no text or accessories.
